This is the official **Boosting Creator** documentation.

Boosting Creator turns car theft into a full job. Players open a laptop, join the queue and get contracts by class: find the parked car, break in and hotwire it, shake off a GPS tracker and an armed crew, then deliver it to a buyer, scratch the VIN to keep it, strip it at a chop shop, or store it in their own garage to sell later. Everything is set up in game from one admin panel, with no restart.

## What it does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contracts by class" icon="ranking-star">
    Classes D to S unlocked with reputation, each with its own vehicles, pay, time limit, minimum police and risks.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Theft and tracker" icon="satellite-dish">
    Break in, hotwire with a minigame, then hack the GPS tracker while the police follow its pings.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Guards and crew chase" icon="user-ninja">
    Armed guards around the car and chasing cars with gunmen once it is stolen.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Fixers" icon="envelope">
    Named buyers who write by email, ask for a clean car, a respray or tuning, and trust the thief more with every job.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Crews" icon="users">
    Up to 4 players share one contract, with the pay split and a bonus for the crew.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Garages and car carrier" icon="warehouse">
    Store stolen cars in a 6-car garage and sell them one by one or all together on a car carrier.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
